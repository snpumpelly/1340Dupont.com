The Building

A century of
character on
O Street

Built in the early 1900s, 1340 21st Street NW is a beautifully preserved residential building nestled on one of Washington's most storied blocks. O Street's iconic tree-lined streetscape and row house architecture have made it a landmark of DC's historic Dupont Circle neighborhood.

  • Classic DC architecture with original period details
  • 10 distinct residences — studios, 1BDs, and 2BDs
  • Furnished and unfurnished options available
  • Locally owned & self-managed by P1 Management
  • Steps from Rock Creek Park, Embassy Row & Dupont Circle
  • Perfect for interns, grad students, law students & young professionals

The Neighborhood

Everything DC
at your doorstep

Dupont Circle is DC's most walkable, livable neighborhood — a favorite of grad students, young professionals, and interns who want to be at the center of it all.

Dupont Circle Metro

Red Line access to the entire Metro system — Georgetown, Capitol Hill, Union Station, and DCA all reachable without a car.

~3 min walk

Rock Creek Park

One of DC's great urban green spaces — running trails, biking paths, and wooded escapes just blocks away.

~5 min walk

Embassy Row

Massachusetts Avenue's world-famous Embassy Row runs right past the building. International culture and stunning architecture around every corner.
